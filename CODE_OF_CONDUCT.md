# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone regardless of
experience, identity, background, ability, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss technical ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Give actionable review feedback with clear priority and rationale.
- Acknowledge uncertainty and correct mistakes openly.
- Respect disclosure boundaries for security reports.
- Keep issue discussions relevant and avoid repetitive disruption.
- Credit contributors and upstream work accurately.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing,
publishing private information, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption,
and retaliation are not accepted in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to repository maintainers. Maintainers may edit
or remove content, warn participants, temporarily restrict participation, or ban
accounts. Reports are handled as confidentially as practical, with conflicts of
interest disclosed and delegated.

This policy applies in repository discussions, reviews, release coordination,
and public representation of the project.
